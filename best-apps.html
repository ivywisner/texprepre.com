First, Sort the Products

"Exam Prep" Is Three Different Jobs

Job one: the required 180 hours. Only a TREC-approved school can do this — no prep app replaces it. Job two: instruction — video lessons, audio courses, live tutoring for people who want to be taught, not just tested. Job three: exam readiness — drilling realistic questions for both sections of the TREC exam until your score is a fact instead of a feeling. Most "best exam prep" lists mix all three jobs into one ranking, which is how a 180-hour school ends up compared against a $20 practice test. Sort by job first and the choice gets easy.

The Other Jobs, Done Well

Where a Different Tool Is the Right Answer

Best for the required 180 hours · online

AceableAgent

If you don't have your six TREC courses yet, you need a school first — and Aceable is the standout online one: an Austin company with modern software, plain-English writing, and courses that run on your phone. Their packages bundle their own exam prep on top. (Going this route? Our Texas school guide has the cost math, and here's the 30% discount — referral link, disclosed in full there.)

Best for the required 180 hours · classroom

Champions School of Real Estate

The Texas institution. If you learn best in a room with an instructor and want the biggest alumni network in Texas real estate, Champions is the classroom route — campuses across the state and decades of licensees behind them. You pay classroom prices and drive to class, and plenty of Texans consider it worth both.

Best for instruction: video, audio & live help

PrepAgent

If you want to be taught — video lessons, audio courses for the commute, live webinars with instructors — PrepAgent is the strongest instruction platform, and its national-portion material runs deep. It's subscription-priced by the week or month and works in the browser or on your phone, so it suits a defined study window more than open-ended drilling. PrepAgent is part of the Aceable family, so the same 30% discount applies here too (referral link — we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you).

Best for readiness metrics

CompuCram

CompuCram's signature is a readiness indicator that rolls your vocabulary, practice, and exam scores into one "are you ready?" percentage. If a data dashboard is what gets you studying, that's their lane — browser-based, sold as a package with a fixed access window, so check the current terms fit your timeline.

Best for a dress rehearsal of the real interface

Pearson VUE's Official Practice Test

The exam provider itself sells a Texas practice test — about $20 for a single sitting. It's one test, not a question bank, so it can't be your study plan — but taken once, near the end of your prep, it's the cheapest way to feel the real testing interface before exam day. Good advice no matter whose app you drill with.
