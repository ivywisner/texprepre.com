Frequently Asked Questions

Is TexPrep RE free to use?
Yes! TexPrep RE is free to download and includes 3 quiz sessions, 1 practice exam, daily questions, all flashcards, and streak tracking at no cost. Pro and Premium upgrades unlock unlimited access and are one-time purchases — no subscriptions required for Pro.
How do I restore my previous purchase?
Open the app and go to the paywall screen (tap any locked feature). Scroll to the bottom and tap "Restore previous purchase." Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID you used to purchase.
Is this app specific to Texas?
Yes — TexPrep RE is built exclusively for the Texas TREC real estate salesperson exam. Every question covers Texas state law and the national topics tested by TREC. It is not a generic national exam prep app.
What does Premium include that Pro doesn't?
Premium adds the full timed simulation — 125 questions with a 4-hour countdown timer, replicating real exam conditions. Pro includes everything else: unlimited quizzes, unlimited practice exams, review missed questions, and exam history.
What is the Premium Weekly subscription?
Premium is available as a $21.99 one-time purchase or a $5.99/week subscription. The subscription auto-renews weekly and can be cancelled anytime in your iPhone's App Store settings under Subscriptions.
How do daily questions work?
Once you've used your 3 free quiz sessions, a Daily Questions mode unlocks automatically. You get 5 fresh questions every day pulled from across all topic areas. They reset at midnight and don't count against your free session limit.
How accurate are the practice questions?
All 1,900+ questions are written specifically for the Texas TREC salesperson exam and cover the same topic areas and question types you'll see on test day — including both Texas state and national content.
Is TexPrep RE available on Android?
TexPrep RE is currently iOS only. An Android version may be available in the future.
